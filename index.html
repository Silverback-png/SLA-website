Who We Are

A Christ-centred community where healing happens.

We believe recovery flourishes in the right environment. Our Christian recovery community gives you structure without pressure, and independence without isolation — a calm, welcoming space where you can focus on your relationship with God, yourself and others.

Through faith, respect, accountability and community, we help you build the life skills and emotional resilience needed for lasting recovery. Wherever you are on your journey, you'll be met with dignity, compassion and encouragement.

What We Offer

Support that fits around your journey.

Comfortable Accommodation

A safe, clean and welcoming home. Furnished rooms, shared living spaces and all the essentials — a calm base where you can rest, recharge and focus on your wellbeing.

Recovery Coaching

Your own coach to walk the road with you. Together you'll set goals, build healthy routines and stay accountable — with encouragement every step of the way.

Community & Wellbeing

You're not doing this alone. House check-ins, shared meals, group activities and peer support — a community that celebrates every win with you, big or small.
